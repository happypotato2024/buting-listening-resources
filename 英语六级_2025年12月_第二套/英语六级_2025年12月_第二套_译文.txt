讲座一

[p35] 原文：Research recently carried out by my team suggests that fish may be self-aware. A certain species of fish recently passed the well-known mirror test—this is a test that demonstrates self-recognition in the animals. Only a few animals, such as humans, apes, dolphins and some birds can recognise themselves in a mirror, and only these animals capable of self-recognition will attempt to remove marks placed on their skin after noticing them in their reflection. This ability suggests the level of higher intelligence and awareness. This is not shown by children until around 18 months. Until now, no fish has demonstrated the ability, but our experiments show the fish in question will scrape up against hard surfaces to remove marks placed on its body by our researchers after viewing itself in a mirror.
[p35] 译文：我的团队最近进行的研究表明鱼可能具有自我意识。某种鱼类最近通过了著名的镜子测试——这是一项展示动物自我识别能力的测试。只有少数动物，例如人类、猿、海豚和一些鸟类可以在镜子中认出自己，并且只有这些具有自我识别能力的动物在注意到镜子中的自己后，才会尝试去除皮肤上的标记。这种能力表明更高的智力和意识水平。儿童直到 18 个月左右才会表现出这一点。到目前为止，还没有鱼表现出这种能力，但我们的实验表明，这种鱼在镜子中观察自己后，会刮擦坚硬的表面，以去除我们的研究人员在其身体上留下的痕迹。

[p36] 原文：The behaviours we observe leave little doubt that this fish behaviourally fulfils all criteria of the mirror test as originally laid out. The fish in our study never attempted to remove the marks when no mirror was present or when the marks were just placed on the mirror. Unmarked fish also did not attempt to remove marks from themselves when interacting with a marked fish on the other side of a window.
[p36] 译文：我们观察到的行为毫无疑问表明这条鱼的行为符合最初制定的镜子测试的所有标准。当没有镜子存在或标记刚刚放在镜子上时，我们研究中的鱼从未尝试去除标记。当与窗户另一侧有标记的鱼互动时，未标记的鱼也不会尝试去除自己的标记。

[p37] 原文：These results show either that fish are self-aware or that the mirror test, which is considered to be the gold standard test used to denote higher intelligence, may be flawed. What is less clear is whether these behaviours should be considered as evidence that fish are self-aware, even though in the past, these same behaviours have been interpreted as self-awareness in so many other animals. Depending on your position, you might reject the interpretation that these behaviours in a fish satisfy passing the test at all. But on what objective basis can you do this when the behaviours they show are so functionally similar to those of other species that have passed the test?
[p37] 译文：这些结果表明，要么鱼具有自我意识，要么镜子测试（被认为是用来表示较高智力的黄金标准测试）可能存在缺陷。不太清楚的是，这些行为是否应该被视为鱼类具有自我意识的证据，尽管在过去，这些相同的行为在许多其他动物中被解释为自我意识。根据您的立场，您可能会拒绝认为鱼的这些行为根本满足通过测试的解释。但是，当它们表现出的行为在功能上与通过测试的其他物种如此相似时，你能在什么客观基础上做到这一点呢？

[p38] 原文：Results from our research raise more questions than answers. What if self-awareness develops like an onion, building layer upon layer rather than appearing all at once? Perhaps, in order to explore self-awareness further, we should stop looking at responses to the mirror as a decisive test. Only with a richer theory of the self and a larger test battery will we be able to determine all of the various levels of self-awareness, including where exactly fish fit in.
[p38] 译文：我们的研究结果提出的问题多于答案。如果自我意识像洋葱一样发展，一层又一层地发展而不是一下子全部出现呢？也许，为了进一步探索自我意识，我们应该停止将对镜子的反应视为决定性的测试。只有拥有更丰富的自我理论和更大的测试电池，我们才能确定所有不同层次的自我意识，包括鱼到底适合什么。

[p41] 原文：Previous studies of young children have suggested that electronic books, which incorporate extra features such as dictionaries and images that depict story events, may have advantages over print. [p41] 译文：之前对幼儿的研究表明，电子书可能比印刷书更具优势，因为电子书包含额外的功能，例如描述故事事件的词典和图像。事实上，多项研究发现，电子书对故事理解和词汇量增长的支持超过了印刷书籍。但也有研究表明，孩子们有时在电子书学习上投入的精力较少，而父母在分享此类书籍时也较少谈论内容。

[p42] 原文：The study we're looking at today aims to give us a better understanding of how good electronic books are for young children. For this study, researchers videoed children aged 17 to 26 months reading with one of their parents. Each parent and child pair was randomly assigned to read either two educational electronic books or two printed books with the same content. While the parent read the words in the printed books aloud, the electronic books had audio of voice actors reading the text.
[p42] 译文：我们今天看到的研究旨在让我们更好地了解电子书对幼儿的好处。在这项研究中，研究人员拍摄了 17 至 26 个月大的儿童与其中一位父母一起阅读的视频。每对父母和孩子被随机分配阅读两本教育电子书或两本内容相同的印刷书籍。当家长大声朗读印刷书籍中的文字时，电子书中有配音演员朗读文字的音频。

[p43] 原文：The researchers found that parents who read the printed books pointed more frequently to pages than parents who read the electronic books, but the opposite was true for the children. Children who read the electronic books also commented more on the content and learned more new words than the children who read printed books. This greater learning is ascribed to greater engagement with the electronic books.
[p43] 译文：研究人员发现，阅读纸质书的父母比阅读电子书的父母更频繁地指向页面，但孩子的情况恰恰相反。阅读电子书的孩子比阅读纸质书的孩子对内容的评论更多，学到的新单词也更多。这种更大的学习归因于对电子书的更多参与。

[p44] 原文：It's important to note that the electronic books in the study were simple. While they included background music, animation and sound effects, there were no other extra features. It's also important to note that there's research on older children finding that electronic books don't always bolster learning. Parents and educators need to be aware extra features in electronic books may increase children's participation with them. However, if these features don't draw attention to the educational content, they may not serve as a supportive feature.
[p44] 译文：值得注意的是，研究中的电子书很简单。虽然它们包括背景音乐、动画和音效，但没有其他额外的功能。同样重要的是要注意，对年龄较大的孩子的研究发现，电子书并不总是能促进学习。家长和教育工作者需要意识到电子书中的额外功能可能会增加孩子们的参与度。然而，如果这些功能不能引起人们对教育内容的关注，它们可能就不能作为支持性功能。
