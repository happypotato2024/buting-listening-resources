这种情况也发生在艺术领域，作家、作曲家和画家在自己的作品中使用以前艺术家的作品。

[p29s7]
The human brain works from precedent.
人脑的运作遵循先例。

[p29s8]
We take the ideas we've inherited and put them together into some new shape.
我们继承了我们继承的想法，并将它们组合成某种新的形式。

[p30s0]
What is a true creator?
什么是真正的创造者？

[p30s1]
Is a creator a genius who makes something out of nothing?
创造者是无中生有的天才吗？

[p30s2]
No.
不。

[p30s3]
Creators are simply humans who use what they inherit, who absorb the past and manipulate it to create possible futures.
创造者只不过是人类，他们利用自己继承的东西，吸收过去并操纵它来创造可能的未来。

[p30s4]
Thus, humans are creators as a rule rather than as an exception.
因此，人类通常是创造者，而不是例外。

[p31s0]
Many dog owners would tell you that their dog somehow knows when they're ill or upset, and according to researchers who study dog cognition, those pet owners are right.
许多狗主人会告诉你，他们的狗以某种方式知道他们什么时候生病或心烦意乱，根据研究狗认知的研究人员的说法，这些宠物主人是对的。

[p31s1]
Dogs do know when their human companions are having a rough time.
狗确实知道它们的人类伙伴何时遇到困难。

[p31s2]
Not only can your dog sense when you have a cold, but domestic dogs have shown an aptitude for detecting both much smaller mood fluctuations and far more serious physical conditions.
当你感冒时，你的狗不仅能感觉到，而且家养的狗也表现出了检测小得多的情绪波动和更严重的身体状况的能力。

[p31s3]
This is because dogs are extremely sensitive to changes in the people they're familiar with, and illness causes change.
这是因为狗对熟悉的人的变化极其敏感，而疾病会导致变化。

[p31s4]
If a person is infected with a virus or bacteria, for example, their odor will be abnormal, and dogs are able to smell that change even if the human can't, because dogs have a much more powerful sense of smell than humans.
例如，如果一个人感染了病毒或细菌，他们的气味就会变得异常，即使人类闻不到，狗也能闻到这种变化，因为狗的嗅觉比人类强大得多。

[p32s0]
Researchers have also found that a person's mood, which can be an indicator of a larger illness, triggers a dog's sense of smell.
研究人员还发现，人的情绪可以触发狗的嗅觉，而情绪可以预示更大的疾病。

[p32s1]
Human emotions manifest physically in chemical signals that are emitted by the body, and dogs are able to smell those as well.
人类的情绪以身体发出的化学信号的形式表现出来，狗也能闻到这些信号。

[p32s2]
Beyond smell, dogs gather information from a person's voice in order to sense changes.
除了嗅觉之外，狗还从人的声音中收集信息以感知变化。

[p33s0]
In 2014, researchers discovered that dogs have an area of the brain similar to one in humans that allows them to understand emotional cues in the tone of a speaker's voice, beyond what they'd be able to pick up from familiar words alone.
2014年，研究人员发现，狗的大脑中有一个与人类相似的区域，可以让它们理解说话者语气中的情感暗示，而不仅仅是它们仅从熟悉的单词中获得的信息。

[p33s1]
A person's voice can also carry indicators of illness.
一个人的声音也可能带有疾病迹象。

[p33s2]
What's not understood quite so well is what dogs understand about these changes.
但人们不太清楚狗对这些变化的理解。

[p33s3]
Humans send out lots of cues, but whether dogs know some of these cues mean illness isn't clear.
人类发出大量信号，但狗是否知道其中一些信号是否意味着疾病尚不清楚。

[p33s4]
What we perceive as concern on a dog's part might be more like increased curiosity or suspicion that something is wrong with us, and sticking close by is a great way to gather more information about the situation.
我们认为狗的担忧可能更像是好奇心增加或怀疑我们出了什么问题，而靠近是收集有关情况的更多信息的好方法。

[p34s0]
Some researchers assert dogs will one day help doctors diagnose diseases, as some dogs have already demonstrated the ability to detect an assortment of ailments, including diabetes and certain types of cancer.
一些研究人员断言，狗有一天会帮助医生诊断疾病，因为一些狗已经表现出检测各种疾病的能力，包括糖尿病和某些类型的癌症。

[p34s1]
But those researchers concede that's probably in the distant future.
但这些研究人员承认这可能是在遥远的未来。

[p34s2]
For now, research suggests dog ownership can have an array of benefits in and of itself.
目前，研究表明养狗本身可以带来一系列好处。

[p34s3]
Keeping a pet dog has been shown to bolster health and boost mood.
饲养宠物狗已被证明可以促进健康并改善情绪。

[p34s4]
Dogs also help people relax, and they can be a particular comfort to those with chronic diseases.
狗还可以帮助人们放松，对于患有慢性疾病的人来说，它们可以带来特别的安慰。

[p35s0]
Earlier this month, the think-tank called Onward published a report, "A Question of Degree", which argues that degrees in the creative arts are not good value for money.
本月早些时候，名为“Onward”的智库发表了一份报告《学位问题》，该报告认为创意艺术学位物有所值。

[p35s1]
Ministers, according to Onward, should "crack down on courses that offer extremely limited value for money to students ten years after graduation", restricting the ability of such courses to recruit new students, if the average graduate earns below the student loans payment threshold.
Onward表示，如果毕业生的平均收入低于学生贷款支付门槛，部长们应该“严厉打击那些在毕业十年后向学生提供的性价比极其有限的课程”，限制此类课程招收新生的能力。

[p35s2]
Courses like science, technology, engineering, and math, and economics, where the average graduate earns a lot, should be favored.
科学、技术、工程、数学和经济学等毕业生平均收入较高的课程应该受到青睐。

[p36s0]
The report provides insight into a government review which looks at how to reform technical education and how to ensure students get good value for money.
该报告提供了对政府审查的深入了解，该审查着眼于如何改革技术教育以及如何确保学生获得物有所值的教育。

[p36s1]
At first glance, it might even seem like Onward have a point.
乍一看，《Onward》似乎有道理。

[p36s2]
According to their data, the majority of creative arts students earn less than 25,000 pounds a year, ten years after graduation.
根据他们的数据，毕业十年后，大多数创意艺术专业的学生年收入不到 25,000 英镑。

[p36s3]
The average male creative arts students, indeed, apparently earn much less than they would, had they simply never gone to university.
事实上，平均男性创意艺术学生的收入显然比他们从未上过大学的情况要少得多。

[p36s4]
This isn't really good for anyone, and it's certainly no good for graduates, who are forced to endure a lifestyle where they can never save up, never buy a house, never hope to retire.
这对任何人都没有好处，对毕业生来说当然也没有好处，他们被迫忍受永远无法存钱、永远无法买房、永远无法退休的生活方式。

[p37s0]
Onward have identified a real problem.
接下来发现了一个真正的问题。

[p37s1]
Creative arts graduates from top universities — like Oxford, with a high proportion of privately-educated students — have fairly good work prospects.
来自顶尖大学（如牛津大学，私立教育学生比例很高）的创意艺术毕业生拥有相当好的工作前景。

[p37s2]
Well, 40 percent of all graduates — regardless of their degree — are on less than 25,000 pounds a year, 5 years after graduation.
好吧，40% 的毕业生（无论其学位如何）在毕业 5 年后每年的收入不到 25,000 英镑。

[p37s3]
This suggests that the problem isn't really to do with specific students studying specific degrees, but really with the economy as a whole.
这表明问题实际上并不与学习特定学位的特定学生有关，而是与整个经济有关。

[p37s4]
Regardless of what they've studied, young people find it hard to get ahead unless they're lucky enough to be born with successful parents.
无论年轻人学什么，除非他们足够幸运，拥有成功的父母，否则他们很难取得成功。

[p38s0]
If ministers want to make education pay for young people, they need to look beyond the higher education sector, towards the wider world.
如果部长们想让教育为年轻人买单，他们就需要把目光超越高等教育领域，放眼更广阔的世界。

[p38s1]
The rewards that education gives us are not measurable.
教育给我们带来的回报是无法衡量的。

[p38s2]
They are not always instantly obvious, and certainly not always direct.
它们并不总是显而易见的，当然也不总是直接的。

[p38s3]
An education makes you a different person from the one you would have been if you hadn't received it.
教育使你成为一个与没有接受教育时不同的人。

[p38s4]
We need to look at the value of education not in the context of a bank balance, but of a life.
我们需要审视教育的价值，而不是从银行余额的角度，而是从生命的角度。

[p38s5]
If we continue to allow ourselves to be distracted with talk of "value for money", we will all be made poorer as a result.
如果我们继续让自己因谈论“物有所值”而分心，结果我们都会变得更穷。
