[p15] 原文：Weather is a constant force in our lives, but there is little marketing research on how it affects businesses. That is, they're willing to pay more money for them. But the correlation is only found with products related to being outside.
[p15] 译文：天气是我们生活中持续存在的力量，但关于它如何影响企业的营销研究却很少。现在，一项新的研究揭示了阳光明媚和下雪的条件如何影响消费者的行为。这些天气条件会促使消费者在脑海中想象使用与相应天气相关的产品。这导致消费者对这些产品赋予更高的价值。也就是说，他们愿意为他们付出更多的钱。但这种相关性只存在于与户外相关的产品中。

[p16] 原文：How does this work? Researchers give the example of a beach towel. On a sunny day, consumers who see that product are not just looking at the towel itself, they are likely imagining themselves lying on the towel in the sun. This mental picture of using the towel increases the value of the product in the consumers' mind.
[p16] 译文：这是如何运作的？研究人员举了沙滩巾的例子。在阳光明媚的日子里，消费者看到该产品时不仅仅是看到毛巾本身，他们很可能会想象自己躺在毛巾上晒太阳。这种使用毛巾的心理画面增加了产品在消费者心目中的价值。

[p17] 原文：Researchers put forward the following hypothesis to explain their findings. Researchers assert this is because there aren't many activities that are enabled by rain. Most products associated with rain, like umbrellas, are only used for protection from the weather, and not for any activities. Researchers believe that companies that sell a wide array of products online can benefit most from the insights this study provides. Online sellers often use complex mathematical formulas to determine what products to feature and how to price these products. Incorporating more data about weather would allow them to make better decisions. This could bolster sales.
[p17] 译文：研究人员提出以下假设来解释他们的发现。他们认为，脑海中的画面在阳光和雪中有效，因为这些天气条件与户外活动有积极的联系。下雨天气看不到这种效果。研究人员断言，这是因为没有多少活动可以通过降雨进行。大多数与雨有关的产品，例如雨伞，仅用于抵御天气，而不用于任何活动。研究人员认为，在线销售各种产品的公司可以从这项研究提供的见解中受益最多。在线卖家经常使用复杂的数学公式来确定要展示哪些产品以及如何为这些产品定价。纳入更多有关天气的数据将使他们能够做出更好的决策。这可以提振销售。

[p18] 原文：Setting the office air-conditioning at about 22℃ has become standard practice across the world. Numerous guidebooks across the world on heating, ventilation and air-conditioning claim office performance peaks at 22 degrees. Many people indeed find relief from soaring summer temperatures in air-conditioned offices. But recent studies have challenged the accepted wisdom that a cool office is more productive.
[p18] 译文：将办公室空调设置在22℃左右已成为世界各地的标准做法。世界各地许多有关供暖、通风和空调的指南都声称办公室性能的峰值温度为 22 度。许多人确实在装有空调的办公室里缓解了夏季飙升的气温。但最近的研究对“凉爽的办公室效率更高”这一公认观点提出了挑战。

[p19] 原文：The reality is more complex. Researchers conducted a review of all studies relating to air-conditioning and productivity. They found that 22 degrees was probably a little chill, even at the height of summer. For a person dressed in typical summer clothing, an optimal range would be between 23 and 26 degrees. And people can even tolerate temperatures beyond this comfort zone as long as they can adjust their clothing and expectations. In fact, even on very hot days, it makes sense to turn the air-conditioning up. People often chase just one optimum temperature, and this is understandable when people feel hot. But there is a range of at least three to four degrees which does not have any adverse impact.
[p19] 译文：现实情况更为复杂。研究人员对所有与空调和生产力相关的研究进行了回顾。他们发现，即使在盛夏，22 度也可能有点冷。对于穿着典型夏季服装的人来说，最佳范围是 23 到 26 度之间。人们甚至可以忍受超出这个舒适区的温度，只要他们能够调整自己的衣服和期望。事实上，即使在非常热的日子里，打开空调也是有意义的。人们常常只追求一种最佳温度，当人们感到热时，这是可以理解的。但至少有三到四度的范围，不会产生任何不利影响。

[p20] 原文：Another issue related to this is that people can become psychologically dependent on air conditioning. If they're used to the environment which is air-conditioned, they tend to prefer lower temperatures. But the studies found that almost all humans became accustomed to the new temperature. It was only at the extreme ends of the temperature range where people's productivity suffered. This range was above 26 degrees and below 19 degrees.
[p20] 译文：与此相关的另一个问题是人们可能会对空调产生心理依赖。如果他们习惯了有空调的环境，他们往往更喜欢较低的温度。但研究发现，几乎所有人类都已经习惯了新的温度。只有在温度范围的极端情况下，人们的生产力才会受到影响。该范围高于 26 度且低于 19 度。

讲座一

[p21] 原文：Psychology research has tended to portray solitude as a negative experience. Studies conducted in the 1970s and 1990s suggested that people felt less happy when alone as compared to being with others. However, a new paper shows an alternative view of solitude, one in which solitude can be positive.
[p21] 译文：心理学研究倾向于将孤独描述为一种消极的经历。 20 世纪 70 年代和 90 年代进行的研究表明，与与他人在一起相比，人们独处时感到不那么快乐。然而，一篇新论文提出了另一种关于孤独的观点，其中孤独可以是积极的。

[p22] 原文：Let's start by looking at the earlier research. It had a couple of shortcomings. First, it measured emotion on a scale from positive to negative, overlooking the possibility that our positive and negative emotions can fluctuate independently. Also, it categorized emotions as simply positive or negative. It didn't consider that emotions arouse us to different degrees, and that both positive and negative emotions can arouse us a lot or a little. That is, whether positive or negative, emotions can be either high-arousal or low-arousal. High-arousal emotions include excitement on the positive side or anger on the negative side, while low-arousal ones include feeling calm on the positive side or lonely on the negative.
[p22] 译文：让我们首先看看早期的研究。它有一些缺点。首先，它以从积极到消极的尺度来衡量情绪，忽略了我们的积极情绪和消极情绪可以独立波动的可能性。此外，它将情绪简单地分为积极或消极。它没有考虑到情绪对我们的唤起程度不同，积极和消极的情绪都可以或多或少地唤起我们。也就是说，无论是积极的还是消极的，情绪可以是高唤醒的，也可以是低唤醒的。高唤醒情绪包括积极的兴奋或消极的愤怒，而低唤醒的情绪包括积极的平静或消极的孤独。

[p23] 原文：This new research attempted to overcome these shortcomings. Researchers began with a simple study. They asked participants to spend 15 minutes sitting alone without engaging in any activity, and measured how this solitude influences their emotional state. This experiment specifically aimed to determine the effect of solitude on high-arousal emotions. It looked at positive emotions such as being excited or interested, and negative emotions including being scared or distressed. The results were clear. After 15 minutes of solitude, the participants showed reductions in both types of emotion.
[p23] 译文：这项新研究试图克服这些缺点。研究人员从一项简单的研究开始。他们要求参与者独自坐着 15 分钟，不进行任何活动，并测量这种孤独如何影响他们的情绪状态。该实验的具体目的是确定独处对高唤起情绪的影响。它着眼于积极情绪，例如兴奋或感兴趣，以及消极情绪，包括害怕或苦恼。结果很明显。经过 15 分钟的独处后，参与者的两种情绪均有所下降。

[p24] 原文：A second study measured the effects of solitude on low-arousal emotions. These included both positive and negative emotions, such as feeling calm, relaxed, sad or lonely. That experiment found that all of these emotions were increased by time alone. Thus it seems past depictions of solitude were wrong. It doesn't have a simple emotional effect that can be characterized as good or bad. Rather, it changes the intensity of our inner experience. It amplifies quieter emotions, but it diminishes the intensity of stronger feelings.
[p24] 译文：第二项研究测量了孤独对低唤醒情绪的影响。这些情绪包括积极和消极的情绪，例如感到平静、放松、悲伤或孤独。该实验发现，所有这些情绪都会随着时间的推移而增加。因此，过去对孤独的描述似乎是错误的。它不具有简单的可以被描述为好或坏的情感效应。相反，它改变了我们内心体验的强度。它会放大较平静的情绪，但会减弱较强烈情绪的强度。

[p25] 原文：It's worth clarifying that these findings relate to relatively brief periods of solitude. This is distinct from prolonged loneliness. Research has demonstrated that the latter is correlated with an assortment of negative physical and psychological effects. How can people benefit from being alone? The findings here suggest that people can use solitude to regulate their emotions. Solitude can help us become quiet after excitement, calm after an angry episode, or simply feel at peace.
[p25] 译文：值得澄清的是，这些发现与相对短暂的独处时间有关。这与长期的孤独不同。研究表明，后者与一系列负面的身体和心理影响相关。人们如何从独处中受益？这里的研究结果表明，人们可以利用独处来调节自己的情绪。独处可以帮助我们在兴奋之后变得安静，在愤怒之后变得平静，或者只是感到平静。

讲座二

[p26] 原文：In 1984, the World Chess Championship was called off abruptly, due to the withered frame of a player who was competing for the title. He wasn't alone in experiencing the extreme physical effects of the game. Elite players can reportedly burn up to an absurd 6,000 calories in one day. Does that mean that thinking harder is a simple route to losing weight?
[p26] 译文：1984年，国际象棋世界锦标赛因一名参赛选手体格枯萎而突然取消。他并不是唯一一个经历过游戏带来的极端身体影响的人。据报道，精英运动员一天可以燃烧多达 6,000 卡路里的热量。这是否意味着更加努力地思考是减肥的简单途径？

[p27] 原文：Well, when the body is at rest, we know that the brain uses up a startling 20% to 25% of the body's overall energy. This level of utilization actually makes the brain the most energy-expensive organ in the body, and yet it makes up only 2% of the body's weight overall.
[p27] 译文：嗯，当身体休息时，我们知道大脑会消耗身体总能量的 20% 到 25%，令人吃惊。这种利用水平实际上使大脑成为身体中最消耗能量的器官，但它只占身体总重量的 2%。

[p28] 原文：So the more we put this organ to work, the more calories we'll burn? Technically, the answer is yes, for cognitively difficult tasks. What counts as a "difficult" mental task varies between individuals. But generally, it could be described as something that the brain cannot solve easily using previously learned routines, or tasks that change the conditions continuously.
[p28] 译文：那么我们越让这个器官工作，我们燃烧的卡路里就越多？从技术上讲，对于认知困难的任务来说，答案是肯定的。什么算作“困难”的脑力任务因人而异。但一般来说，它可以被描述为大脑无法使用先前学习的例程或不断改变条件的任务轻松解决的问题。

[p29] 原文：However, deep thinking will not burn off the calories gained from eating a sugary snack. Because in relation to the brain's huge overall energy usage, which is devoted to a multitude of tasks, the energy required just to think harder is actually comparatively tiny. We're unconscious of most of what uses up the brain's energy. A lot of that activity is unrelated to conscious activities like learning how to sing or play the guitar.
[p29] 译文：然而，深度思考并不能消耗掉吃含糖零食所获得的热量。因为相对于大脑用于执行多项任务的巨大总体能量消耗而言，仅仅更加努力地思考所需的能量实际上相对较小。我们对大部分消耗大脑能量的事物都没有意识到。其中很多活动与学习如何唱歌或弹吉他等有意识的活动无关。

[p30] 原文：The brain is able to allocate blood (and thus energy) to particular regions that are being active at that point. But the overall energy availability in the brain is thought to be constant. So, while there might be significant increases in energy use at localized regions of the brain when we perform difficult cognitive tasks, when it comes to the whole brain's energy budget overall, these activities don't significantly alter it.
[p30] 译文：大脑能够将血液（以及能量）分配到当时活跃的特定区域。但大脑中可用的总能量被认为是恒定的。因此，虽然当我们执行困难的认知任务时，大脑局部区域的能量消耗可能会显着增加，但当涉及到整个大脑的总体能量预算时，这些活动并不会显着改变它。

[p31] 原文：So, why did the chess champion grow too skinny to compete in his chess competition? The general consensus is that it mostly comes down to stress and reduced food consumption, not mental exhaustion. Keeping your body pumped up for action for longer periods of time is very energy demanding. If you can't eat as often or as much as you can or would normally — then you might lose weight.
[p31] 译文：那么，为什么国际象棋冠军变得太瘦而无法参加国际象棋比赛呢？普遍的共识是，这主要归因于压力和食物消耗减少，而不是精神疲惫。长时间保持身体充满活力是非常需要能量的。如果你不能像平常那样经常吃东西或吃那么多东西——那么你的体重可能会减轻。

It takes hard work and extensive preparation to answer this question well.
