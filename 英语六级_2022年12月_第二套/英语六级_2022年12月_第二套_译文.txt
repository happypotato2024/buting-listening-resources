[p37] 原文：Why do we form opinions or attitudes about someone or something without really knowing much about them? "Pre-" means before and "-judice" refers to judgment. Hence, prejudice means to judge before having adequate knowledge.
[p37] 译文：为什么我们在没有真正了解某人或某事的情况下就对他们形成意见或态度？仅仅听到关于一个人、一个地方或一件事的好或坏就会对我们的看法产生积极或消极的影响。但让别人的意见决定我们的意见是危险的。在真正了解某人或某事之前就对其形成看法称为偏见。 “Pre-”是指之前，“-judice”是指判断。因此，偏见意味着在没有足够的知识之前就做出判断。

[p38] 原文：We can be prejudiced toward or against someone or something. In either case, we are only allowing ourselves to see half of the picture. Very few people or things in this world are all good or all bad. Prejudiced attitudes are usually based on myths, half truths or incorrect information, and they are dangerous because they can keep us from learning the truth about someone or something. People form prejudices against others for many reasons—differences in their race, religion, gender or occupation. Prejudices keep people apart. They keep us from really knowing and understanding each other.
[p38] 译文：我们可能会对某人或某事抱有偏见或反对。无论哪种情况，我们都只允许自己看到一半的图片。这个世界上很少有人或事都是好或都是坏的。偏见态度通常基于神话、半真半假或不正确的信息，它们很危险，因为它们会阻止我们了解某人或某事的真相。人们对他人形成偏见的原因有很多——种族、宗教、性别或职业的差异。偏见使人与人分离。它们使我们无法真正了解和理解彼此。

[p39] 原文：We should feel proud of who we are and the group of people we represent. If feelings of pride begin to turn to feelings of superiority when we think that our group or our beliefs are better than those around us, however, then we begin to develop prejudiced attitudes that can be harmful. For example, the prejudiced attitudes of one group may keep another group from attending certain schools, from living in any neighborhood they want, or from getting a job or a promotion. Extreme feelings of prejudice have caused the deaths of innocent people. We are responsible for our own thoughts and opinions. When we let someone else tell us what to think about someone or something, we are giving up some control of our own lives.
[p39] 译文：我们应该为我们是谁以及我们所代表的群体感到自豪。然而，如果当我们认为我们的群体或我们的信仰比周围的人更好时，自豪感开始转变为优越感，那么我们就会开始形成可能有害的偏见态度。例如，一个群体的偏见态度可能会阻止另一群体就读某些学校，无法住在他们想要的任何社区，或者无法找到工作或升职。极端的偏见情绪造成了无辜者的死亡。我们对自己的想法和意见负责。当我们让别人告诉我们如何看待某人或某事时，我们就放弃了对自己生活的一些控制。

[p40] 原文：Before you form an attitude or opinion, find out for yourself about the person or the thing in question. Sometimes we don't realize that we hold prejudiced attitudes toward or against someone or something. We need to carefully examine our lives and our fears, and to ask ourselves whether our attitudes come from our personal knowledge and experience or from rumors and fear of the unknown. The good news about prejudice is that we are not born with it. Prejudiced attitudes and opinions develop over time. [p40] 译文：在形成态度或观点之前，请自己了解有关的人或事。有时我们没有意识到我们对某人或某事抱有偏见的态度。我们需要仔细审视我们的生活和我们的恐惧，并扪心自问，我们的态度是来自我们的个人知识和经验，还是来自谣言和对未知的恐惧。关于偏见的好消息是我们并非生来就有偏见。偏见的态度和观点随着时间的推移而形成。但通过教育和知识，我们可以用合作和理解来取代偏见。

But letting the opinions of another person determine what our opinions will be is dangerous.
但让别人的意见决定我们的意见是危险的。

[p37s3]
Forming opinions about someone or something before really knowing them well is called prejudice.
在真正了解某人或某事之前就对其形成看法称为偏见。

[p37s4]
"Pre-" means before and "-judice" refers to judgment.
“Pre-”是指之前，“-judice”是指判断。

[p37s5]
Hence, prejudice means to judge before having adequate knowledge.
因此，偏见意味着在没有足够的知识之前就做出判断。

[p38s0]
We can be prejudiced toward or against someone or something.
我们可能会对某人或某事抱有偏见或反对。

[p38s1]
In either case, we are only allowing ourselves to see half of the picture.
无论哪种情况，我们都只允许自己看到一半的图片。

[p38s2]
Very few people or things in this world are all good or all bad.
这个世界上很少有人或事都是好或都是坏的。

[p38s3]
Prejudiced attitudes are usually based on myths, half truths or incorrect information, and they are dangerous because they can keep us from learning the truth about someone or something.
偏见态度通常基于神话、半真半假或不正确的信息，它们很危险，因为它们会阻止我们了解某人或某事的真相。

[p38s4]
People form prejudices against others for many reasons—differences in their race, religion, gender or occupation.
人们对他人形成偏见的原因有很多——种族、宗教、性别或职业的差异。

[p38s5]
Prejudices keep people apart.
偏见使人与人分离。

[p38s6]
They keep us from really knowing and understanding each other.
它们使我们无法真正了解和理解彼此。

[p39s0]
We should feel proud of who we are and the group of people we represent.
我们应该为我们是谁以及我们所代表的群体感到自豪。

[p39s1]
If feelings of pride begin to turn to feelings of superiority when we think that our group or our beliefs are better than those around us, however, then we begin to develop prejudiced attitudes that can be harmful.
然而，如果当我们认为我们的群体或我们的信仰比周围的人更好时，自豪感开始转变为优越感，那么我们就会开始形成可能有害的偏见态度。

[p39s2]
For example, the prejudiced attitudes of one group may keep another group from attending certain schools, from living in any neighborhood they want, or from getting a job or a promotion.
例如，一个群体的偏见态度可能会阻止另一群体就读某些学校，无法住在他们想要的任何社区，或者无法找到工作或升职。

[p39s3]
Extreme feelings of prejudice have caused the deaths of innocent people.
极端的偏见情绪造成了无辜者的死亡。

[p39s4]
We are responsible for our own thoughts and opinions.
我们对自己的想法和意见负责。

[p39s5]
When we let someone else tell us what to think about someone or something, we are giving up some control of our own lives.
当我们让别人告诉我们如何看待某人或某事时，我们就放弃了对自己生活的一些控制。

[p40s0]
Before you form an attitude or opinion, find out for yourself about the person or the thing in question.
在形成态度或观点之前，请自己了解有关的人或事。

[p40s1]
Sometimes we don't realize that we hold prejudiced attitudes toward or against someone or something.
有时我们没有意识到我们对某人或某事抱有偏见的态度。

[p40s2]
We need to carefully examine our lives and our fears, and to ask ourselves whether our attitudes come from our personal knowledge and experience or from rumors and fear of the unknown.
我们需要仔细审视我们的生活和我们的恐惧，并扪心自问，我们的态度是来自我们的个人知识和经验，还是来自谣言和对未知的恐惧。

[p40s3]
The good news about prejudice is that we are not born with it.
关于偏见的好消息是我们并非生来就有偏见。

[p40s4]
Prejudiced attitudes and opinions develop over time.
偏见的态度和观点随着时间的推移而形成。

[p40s5]
But with education and knowledge, we can replace our prejudices with cooperation and understanding.
但通过教育和知识，我们可以用合作和理解来取代偏见。

[p41s0]
When I started high school, it was a shock.
当我开始上高中时，我感到很震惊。

[p41s1]
I had spent eight years fighting my way to be the most popular kid in the Catholic school student body.
我花了八年的时间努力成为天主教学校学生中最受欢迎的孩子。

[p41s2]
I had been a big, tough 8th grader and suddenly I was a lonely 9th grader bullied by the big, tough 12th grade seniors who ran the high school.
我曾经是一个高大、强硬的八年级学生，突然之间，我变成了一个孤独的九年级学生，被管理高中的高大、强硬的十二年级高年级生欺负。

[p41s3]
I realized then that it's nice to strive for something, but that you also have to enjoy the moment you're in and be happy where you are.
那时我意识到，为某件事而努力是件好事，但你也必须享受你所处的时刻，并为你所在的地方感到高兴。

[p41s4]
Rock and roll had always been an important part of my life.
摇滚乐一直是我生活的重要组成部分。

[p41s5]
I remember my friends and I used to drive around until the late hours of the nights listening to the music of Rock and Roll Legends.
我记得我和我的朋友们常常开车到处听摇滚传奇的音乐直到深夜。

[p41s6]
During those teenage years, I built friendships that I thought would last a lifetime.
在青少年时期，我建立了我认为会持续一生的友谊。

[p41s7]
Most people that age think the same thing, but people drift apart.
大多数同龄人的想法都是一样的，但人们却渐行渐远。

[p41s8]
Jobs, families and tragedies separate people from those lasting friendships.
工作、家庭和悲剧使人们与那些持久的友谊分离。

[p42s0]
The tragedy that separated me from my friends forever was the Vietnam war.
使我与朋友永远分离的悲剧是越南战争。

[p42s1]
A year after I graduated from high school, I left for Vietnam.
高中毕业一年后，我前往越南。

[p42s2]
I came back, burned out, and tired, as though I had lived 10 lifetimes in the short span of 14 months, the 14 months I was in the war.
我回来时，精疲力竭，疲惫不堪，仿佛我在短短的 14 个月里活了 10 辈子，而这 14 个月是我在战争中度过的。

[p42s3]
I couldn't relate to the friends I had had in high school.
我无法与高中时的朋友建立联系。

[p42s4]
They still seem childish, concerned with childish things that weren't important to me.
他们看起来仍然很幼稚，关心对我来说不重要的幼稚事情。

[p42s5]
I was still trying to cope with the death, destruction and evil I had seen in Vietnam.
我仍在努力应对我在越南看到的死亡、破坏和邪恶。

[p42s6]
I felt like we had done terrible things to innocent people there and in turn, I had seen terrible things done to my friends.
我觉得我们对那里的无辜人民做了可怕的事情，反过来，我也看到我的朋友们也受到了可怕的事情。

[p43s0]
I withdrew for my friends and started college.
我为了朋友们退出并开始上大学。

[p43s1]
Then I quit college and took many different jobs.
然后我从大学退学并从事了许多不同的工作。

[p43s2]
I spent a lot of my time and money on alcohol and other drugs.
我花了很多时间和金钱在酒精和其他毒品上。

[p43s3]
Finally, in an effort to get my life going in the right direction again, I sold everything and took what little money I had and bought myself an airplane ticket to Israel.
最后，为了让我的生活重新回到正确的方向，我变卖了一切，用仅有的一点钱给自己买了一张飞往以色列的机票。

[p43s4]
I went there to study history.
我去那里学习历史。

[p43s5]
While studying at Haifa University, I met my wife, who was also an American student.
在海法大学学习期间，我认识了我的妻子，她也是一名美国学生。

[p44s0]
I now teach in a high school back in America.
我现在在美国的一所高中教书。

[p44s1]
I look at my students and see them struggling with many of the very things I struggled with many years ago.
我看着我的学生，看到他们正在为许多我多年前所困扰的事情而苦苦挣扎。

[p44s2]
As a teacher, I try to help them over the rough spots as best I can.
作为一名老师，我会尽力帮助他们度过难关。
