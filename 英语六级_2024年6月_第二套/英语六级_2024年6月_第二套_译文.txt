[p31] 原文：If you read an article about a controversial issue, do you think you'd realize if it had changed your beliefs? No one knows your own mind like you do. [p32] 原文：The researchers recruited over 200 undergraduates across two studies and focused on their beliefs about whether physical punishment of kids is an effective form of discipline. [p32] 译文：研究人员在两项研究中招募了 200 多名本科生，重点关注他们对孩子体罚是否是一种有效管教形式的看法。学生们从“1.完全不相信”到“9.完全相信”的范围内报告了他们对体罚是否是管教孩子的有效方法的初步看法。

[p33] 原文：Several weeks later, they were given one of two research-based texts to read. Each was several pages long and either presented the arguments and data in favour of physical punishment or against it. After this, the students answered some questions to test their comprehension and memory of the text. Then, the students again scored their belief in whether physical punishment is effective or not. Finally, the researchers asked them to recall what their belief had been at the start of the study.
[p33] 译文：几周后，他们获得了两本研究性文本中的一本供他们阅读。每篇都有好几页长，要么提出了支持体罚或反对体罚的论点和数据。随后，学生们回答了一些问题，以测试他们对课文的理解和记忆。然后，学生们再次对体罚是否有效的信念进行评分。最后，研究人员要求他们回忆研究开始时的信念。

[p34] 原文：The students' belief about physical punishment changed when they read a text that argued against their own initial position. Crucially, the memory of their initial belief was shifted in the direction of their new belief. In fact, their memory was closer to their current belief than their original belief. The more their belief had changed, the larger this memory bias tended to be, suggesting the students were relying on their current belief to deduce their initial belief. The memory bias was unrelated to the measures of how well they'd understood or recalled the text, suggesting these factors didn't play a role in memory of initial belief or awareness of belief change.
[p34] 译文：当学生们读到一篇反对他们最初立场的文章时，他们对体罚的看法发生了变化。至关重要的是，他们最初信仰的记忆转向了新信仰的方向。事实上，他们的记忆比他们原来的信念更接近他们当前的信念。他们的信念改变得越多，这种记忆偏差往往就越大，这表明学生们正在依靠他们当前的信念来推断他们最初的信念。记忆偏差与他们对文本的理解或回忆程度无关，这表明这些因素在最初信念的记忆或信念改变的意识中没有发挥作用。

[p35] 原文：The researchers concede that this research was about changes to mostly moderate beliefs. [p35] 译文：研究人员承认，这项研究是关于改变大多数温和信念的。在极端或根深蒂固的信念发生变化的背景下，研究结果可能会有所不同。然而，我们对大多数主题的信念都处于中等范围，当我们在日常生活中阅读信息丰富的材料时，这些有趣的发现表明我们大多不知道我们刚刚阅读的内容如何更新和改变了我们自己的立场。
